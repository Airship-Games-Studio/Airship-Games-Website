## How to play

1. **Design.** Orbit with Explore (right-drag pans, the wheel zooms), and sculpt with Raise, Lower, Smooth and Flatten. Place a start, then a finish lower down, or choose **Place a starter course**.
2. **Ride.** It unlocks once both markers are placed.

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Up / Down | Pedal / brake |
| Left / Right | Steer |
| W / S, A / D | Pitch, tilt |
| Space (hold, release) | Charge and jump |
| F / G | Backflip / frontflip |
| Q / E | Spin left / right |
| R / Esc | Restart / back to Design |

Rampage Sandbox needs a desktop browser with WebGL, a keyboard and a mouse. Phones and tablets see a "Best on desktop" message. Your course and best score are saved in your browser.
