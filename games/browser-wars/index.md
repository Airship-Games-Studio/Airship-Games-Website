## How to play

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W / Up | Thrust |
| S / Down | Reverse |
| A, D / Left, Right | Turn |
| Shift | Boost |
| Space | Shoot |
| B | Drop a bomb |
| P / R | Pause / reset your ship |

Fly into a blue whirlpool to travel to that article. On a narrow touch screen, on-screen buttons replace the keys.

## About the content

Browser Wars loads articles live from Wikipedia in your browser, so Wikipedia receives your request (see our [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }})). Article text is adapted from Wikipedia contributors under [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and the game shows that attribution while you play. If Wikipedia can't be reached, a clearly labelled offline sample loads instead. Your chosen home article is saved in your browser.

Browser Wars isn't affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.
