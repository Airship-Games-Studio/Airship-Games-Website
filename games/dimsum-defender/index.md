## How to play

1. **Set up.** Place furniture on the 1-metre grid, or switch to fine placement. Rotate or delete pieces as you go. Put the aquarium and waiting chairs where guests will wait, then use privacy walls and waypoints to shape the kitchen loop.
2. **Start service.** Your furniture is locked in.
3. **Seat guests.** Click a waiting party, then a glowing table that fits them.
4. **Watch the carts.** Guests eat dim sum from carts that pass nearby, then pay and leave. A cart leaves every five seconds; press **Send Bonus Cart** for more sales.
5. **Finish the shift.** Service lasts one minute. When time is up, win by surviving until every cart is back. You lose if more than 100 dim sum go uneaten.

Drag to orbit the camera and scroll to zoom.

## Status

Dim Sum Defender is an early prototype that we're building in the open. It runs in a desktop browser with WebGL, and works best with a mouse.
