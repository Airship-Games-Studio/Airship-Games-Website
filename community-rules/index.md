## Help shape the game {#welcome}

Tell us what happened, what felt good or bad, or what you wish worked differently. A single sentence is enough. You don't need a polished solution, and you don't need to agree with us. Frustration, criticism and unpopular opinions are welcome.

**In short: criticize the game, respect people, protect privacy, and don't manipulate votes.**

## The rules {#rules}

1. **Challenge ideas; don't target people.** No threats, harassment, hate or sexual content involving minors. Don't organize attacks on other players or communities.
2. **Protect people's privacy.** Don't post anyone's personal information, passwords or private messages, including your own.
3. **Keep it relevant and honest.** Post in the right game's space. No spam, scams, impersonation, faked evidence or undisclosed promotion.
4. **Let votes represent people.** No extra accounts, bought or traded votes, voting bots, pressure campaigns or ban evasion. Inviting friends to discuss an idea is fine; manufacturing support isn't.
5. **Respect rights and safety.** Only share material you have the right to share. Report security problems privately to [contact@airshipgames.com](mailto:contact@airshipgames.com), and don't post details that would put other players at risk.

## How feedback becomes development {#development}

Reactions and votes set the default priorities for what we build next. We explain significant departures, for example when a request would change what the game is, or isn't safe, legal, affordable or technically possible. We don't remove criticism because it's inconvenient. Votes on ideas are separate from moderation: a low vote count is never a rule violation.

## Reporting and review {#reporting}

Use **Report** on a post or idea that breaks these rules, or email [contact@airshipgames.com](mailto:contact@airshipgames.com) about a privacy problem or to ask us to review a decision. Feedback isn't an emergency service.

While we look at a report we may hide the content. Depending on what happened we may remove it, warn the author, limit posting or suspend the account, and where practical we'll say which rule applied. Serious threats, exposed personal information or active abuse may be removed straight away.

Acknowledgements and status messages are automated and say so. They aren't a personal review.

## Changes {#changes}

We'll update these rules as the communities grow, change the date at the top and note it below. See also the [Terms of Service]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}) and [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
