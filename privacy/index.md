## Who we are {#who-we-are}

Airship Games is the trading name of **Ministry of Product LLC**, a Washington limited liability company in the United States ("we", "us"). This policy covers airshipgames.com, the games we publish on airshipgames.com subdomains and brand domains (such as dimsumdefender.com), and our in-game Feedback service, called Aerodrome, at aerodrome.airshipgames.com.

## The short version {#summary}

- You can play our games without an account. We don't run ads, sell personal information, or use tracking cookies.
- If you sign in to Feedback, we get your Google account ID, email address and name from Google. Your public display name starts as your Google name (or the first part of your email address), and you can change it any time in **Account**.
- Posts, replies, ideas and your display name are public. Vote totals are public; who voted is not.
- You can delete your Feedback account yourself, with or without your posts, from the Account tab in the panel.
- We don't run AI on your posts and don't use them to train AI models. We'll update this policy before that ever changes.

## This website {#website}

airshipgames.com is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and browser details when you load a page, and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives the same request information. We don't receive or keep those logs.

## Our games {#games}

Our web games run in your browser and don't need an account.

- **Settings and progress** may be saved in your browser's local storage on your device (for example, Function Skatepark's settings and equations). They stay on your device and you can clear them in your browser settings.
- **Hosting.** Game sites are served by Cloudflare, which processes your IP address and browser details to deliver and protect them. Cloudflare may produce cookieless, aggregate visit counts for these sites. We don't use them to identify anyone.
- **Fonts.** Some games load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address and browser details.
- **Browser Wars** loads live articles from Wikipedia. Your browser requests them directly from the Wikimedia Foundation, which receives your IP address and the articles requested under [its own privacy policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy).
- **Help Build the Game.** When you click this link on a brand domain such as dimsumdefender.com, we count the click as an anonymous event (game, hostname and time, plus flags that mark staff and test traffic).

## Feedback (Aerodrome) {#feedback}

The Feedback panel lets you read what other players are saying, post, reply, propose ideas and vote. Reading needs no account; taking part needs a Google sign-in.

| What | Why |
| --- | --- |
| **Google sign-in:** your Google account ID, email address and name. We never see your Google password. | To create your account, sign you in and prevent abuse. |
| **Your profile:** display name (it starts as your Google name, or the first part of your email address if there is none, and you can change it in **Account**), role (player, editor or admin), account status, and when you confirmed the age and public-posting notice. | To show who wrote what, decide what you're allowed to do, and record that you saw the notice. |
| **Invite:** if you join a game's community with an invite code, which code you used and when. | To run the invite-only beta. It's deleted with your account. |
| **Your contributions:** posts, replies, ideas, votes and reports, with times and the game they belong to. | To run the discussion and voting, and to decide what to build next. |
| **Sign-in session:** an essential, secure cookie that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days, plus security tokens. | To keep you signed in and protect your actions from forgery. |
| **Browser storage:** a random tab ID in the game page's session storage, and unsent drafts, the tab ID and the game's hostname in the panel's session storage, all cleared when you close the tab. | So a draft survives signing in, and to count visits without identifying you. |
| **Usage events:** which game and panel version, the page's hostname, the event (for example "panel opened" or "idea posted") and its time, a random per-tab ID, the panel section or game you went to, and whether you were signed in, staff or testing. | To see whether Feedback is used and working. Events never include your posts, email, name, player ID or game state. |
| **Moderation records:** reports (a reason and a link to the reported post), and actions editors take. | To deal with abuse and keep a record of decisions. |
| **Diagnostic logs:** request details such as IP address, browser and errors, kept by our host. | To run, debug and secure the service. |

We don't offer screenshot uploads, community credits or AI summaries yet. We'll update this policy before any of them launch.

## What others can see {#visibility}

Your display name, posts, replies and ideas are public on that game's feedback pages, to anyone, including people who aren't signed in. Your posts may be quoted when the game changes because of them. Vote totals are public, but we don't show who voted. Your email address, Google ID, sign-in details and reports are never public. Others can copy public posts, and deleting them from Feedback can't remove copies made elsewhere.

## Who we share it with {#providers}

We use these service providers to run things for us:

- **Cloudflare** hosts the games and Feedback, stores Feedback data and keeps diagnostic logs.
- **Google** provides sign-in, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **GitHub** hosts this website.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people or the service, or to a buyer of the business under the same protections. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it {#retention}

These are the longest we keep each kind of information. We delete it sooner when we no longer need it.

| Information | How long |
| --- | --- |
| Public posts, replies and ideas | While the game's community runs, unless you delete them. |
| Votes | Until you remove the vote or delete your account. |
| Account details and email | While your account exists. Deleting your account removes them straight away; a request by email is completed within 30 days of verifying it's you. |
| Usage events | 90 days. We may keep totals that can't identify anyone for longer. |
| Diagnostic and security logs | 30 days, unless needed to investigate a specific incident. |
| Moderation reports and action records | 12 months after the report is resolved. |
| Sign-in sessions | Removed once they expire (at most 30 days) or a day after you sign out. |
| Database backups | Our host keeps restore points for 7 days, so deleted information may stay in a backup for that long. If we ever restore one, we delete it again. |

## Deleting your account {#deletion}

In the Feedback panel, open **Account** and choose one of:

- **Delete my account, keep my posts.** We sign you out everywhere, remove your email, Google ID and votes, and show your remaining posts as "Deleted player". If a post itself identifies you, ask us to remove it too.
- **Delete my account and my posts.** We also delete your posts, replies and ideas. Where others replied to your post, a content-free "[deleted]" placeholder keeps their replies readable. An idea that other players voted for, that is on the roadmap, or that other ideas were merged into keeps its title but loses your text.

Deletion happens immediately. Signing in again later creates a new, empty account. You can also ask us to remove a single post without closing your account. Deleting feedback doesn't undo changes already made to a game.

## Your choices and requests {#rights}

Email [privacy@airshipgames.com](mailto:privacy@airshipgames.com) to ask for a copy of your information, to correct it, to delete your account or a particular post, or to use any other privacy right you have where you live. We'll verify it's you through the email address on your account. We'll never ask for your password, and we won't ask for ID documents unless the law requires them. You won't be treated differently for using these rights.

## Automated replies {#automated}

Confirmations the service shows you, for example after you report a post or delete your account, are automated. If we add automated acknowledgements for email requests, they will say so. An automated confirmation means we've received something, not that a person has reviewed it. Replies from editors in a discussion are written by people.

## Age requirements {#age}

You must be at least 13 to sign in to Feedback, or older if the law where you live requires it. If you're under the age of majority where you live, a parent or guardian must agree to your use. Our games themselves need no account. If you believe a child under 13 has signed in, email [privacy@airshipgames.com](mailto:privacy@airshipgames.com) and we'll delete the account.

## Security and location {#security}

We protect Feedback with secure cookies, encrypted connections, forgery protection and access controls, but no online service can be perfectly secure. Report a suspected problem to [contact@airshipgames.com](mailto:contact@airshipgames.com). Our providers process information in the United States and other countries where they operate.

## Changes to this policy {#changes}

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and add a line below. For a significant change, such as a new use of your information, we'll give notice in the Feedback panel before it takes effect, and ask for your agreement where the law requires it.
