Community ideas and votes guide development priorities. The operator remains responsible for preserving the game's identity and handling safety, legal, technical, cost and dependency constraints. A vote or roadmap position is not a guarantee that a feature will be built, built unchanged, or delivered on a particular date. Material departures from community priorities should be explained publicly.

## 5. Game captures and annotations

This does not grant rights in third-party material, other players' personal information or unrelated applications.

Review the final image and remove private information before submission. Your post and attached image are public when the submission screen says they will be public. Do not upload passwords, contact details, private conversations, faces or other personal information about others without appropriate permission. The capture tool's available controls and limitations are explained before submission.

Private identity and moderation information is not made public merely because an assistant can access it.

Automated access is permitted only through an authorized integration and its scopes, limits and security requirements. This does not authorize public scraping, vote manipulation, access to other accounts or reuse of feedback outside approved purposes.
