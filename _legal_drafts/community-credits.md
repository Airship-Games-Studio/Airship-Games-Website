# Community credits

**Review draft, version 0.1 — October 15, 2026. Planned feature; not an available account control or contractual credit guarantee.**

Games grow through reactions from people playing them. We intend to recognize those contributions in a movie-style Community Credits page for each game, with links to shipped changes where appropriate.

Recognition may include ideas and inspiration, design refinement, playtesting, bug discovery, accessibility feedback and community stewardship. A screenshot that reveals a problem can matter as much as a detailed proposal. We do not treat the first post, most votes or most comments as the only evidence of contribution.

## Attribution and choice

We link original feedback to proposals and shipped work. AI may suggest credits from that evidence; an accountable operator checks the attribution before publication. We can acknowledge independent similar ideas and several contributors to one change without declaring an exclusive inventor.

Public credit is opt-in under this proposed policy. Choose an appropriate display name or pseudonym; we do not require a legal name or publish your sign-in email. You can decline credit, change the displayed name, ask for a correction or request removal through [CREDITS CONTACT OR FORM]. We explain disputed credit decisions and allow review. Staff and paid contributors are identified accurately rather than presented as unpaid community members.

## Meaning of recognition

Credits acknowledge participation, not an award of equity, royalties, revenue share, employment, partnership or ownership in a game. Separate written agreements apply if we commission work, offer payment or incorporate creative assets requiring their own licence. We will not invite work on a promise of compensation and then describe it as ordinary unpaid feedback.

We aim to preserve accurate release history, while honoring applicable privacy and removal rights. Attribution errors and omissions can occur and should be corrected. Do not promise automatic or permanent credits until the controls and correction process are implemented.
