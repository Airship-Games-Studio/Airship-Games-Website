## Retention

The operator approved the following schedule on October 15, 2026. [REVIEW DRAFT: implementation and provider verification must precede effectiveness; these are not claims about current deletion jobs.]

| Information | Agreed retention / deletion rule |
| --- | --- |
| Public posts, replies and proposals | While the game's community operates, unless deleted; review archival needs when a game closes. |
| Submitted screenshots | While the associated post remains available; remove attachments when that post is deleted. |
| Votes | While the account and proposal exist; remove the player's votes on account deletion and maintain correct totals. |
| Account details and email | While the account exists; delete within 30 days of a verified deletion request, except narrowly documented legal/security needs. |
| Detailed usage analytics | 90 days from collection, then delete; genuinely de-identified aggregate statistics may be kept longer. |
| Routine diagnostic/security logs | 30 days from collection; retain specific incident evidence longer only for a documented need. |
| Moderation reports and action history | 12 months after resolution; review longer retention for active restrictions or serious repeat abuse. |
| Temporary uploads and abandoned server-side drafts | 24 hours after becoming abandoned; define and test the abandonment trigger. |
| Raw AI/MCP exports | Delete after processing; 30-day maximum for routine working copies. Store useful summaries in Aerodrome rather than accumulating downloads. |
| Backups containing deleted information | Expire within 30 days if selected providers support it; verify actual provider windows before making a public promise and reapply deletions after restore. |
| Community credits | While the game's credits remain available; allow changes or removal of public attribution. |

Document legal/security exceptions with a restricted purpose and review/expiry; they are not indefinite routine retention. Session expiry does not itself purge stored session records. Shorter applicable deadlines take precedence. Remove or correct controlled thumbnails, caches, indexes, identifiable AI derivatives and tracked exports; reapply deletions after backup restoration. Deletion does not undo shipped game changes or guarantee erasure of independent public copies.

## Your choices and requests

Use privacy@airshipgames.com to request access, correction, account deletion, content removal, credit-name changes or removal, and other rights available where you live. We verify identity proportionately without asking for your Google password. Account deletion and content deletion are separate requests; explain the result to the player rather than assuming anonymization removes personal information from post text or images.

Choose **Delete my account** to revoke sign-in, remove account identifiers and votes, and disassociate retained discussion as “Deleted player”; remove identifying text and images as needed, since disassociation alone is not anonymization. Choose **Delete my account and contributions** to remove your posts and images too, leaving minimal placeholders if needed to preserve others' replies. You may request individual content or credit-name removal without closing the account. Shared proposals should be redacted or rewritten without removing unrelated contributions.

Responses to requests are automated and labelled, including acknowledgements and status/completion notices. We do not present a queued request as completed or an automated message as a personal review.
