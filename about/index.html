About Airship Games

A small studio that builds games with their players

Airship Games is run by Ministry of Product LLC, a Washington company doing business as Airship Games. We make small games you can play in your browser, and we build them in the open: players discuss, suggest ideas and vote, and that shapes what we make next.

What we're doing

Most games are built behind closed doors and shown to players at the end. We'd rather put a playable version out early and let the people playing it help decide where it goes. Our games are getting a Feedback panel, starting with Dim Sum Defender: the votes will set our default priorities, and the roadmap will be public.
